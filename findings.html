WCAG Mapping

Governing Accessibility Criteria

  • 1.3

    1.3.1 Info and Relationships (Level A)

    Information, structure, and relationships conveyed through presentation can be programmatically determined (List containers, ARIA parent/child roles).

  • 1.4

    1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (Level AA)

    Text visual presentation must have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text.

  • 2.5

    2.5.5 / 2.5.8 Target Size & Spacing

    Pointer inputs must have adequate touch target dimensions and spacing to prevent accidental activation on touchscreen devices.
