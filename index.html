Audit Workflow

How the Audit Was Conducted

  1. 01

    Automated Lighthouse Scan

    Chrome DevTools Lighthouse was run against India.gov.in evaluating the Accessibility category to capture the baseline score of 86.

  2. 02

    Document & Categorize

    Identified barriers were logged in an issue register with WCAG success criteria mappings and severity classifications.

  3. 03

    Manual Verification Plan

    Automated tools detect ~30-40% of WCAG issues. Manual keyboard navigation and assistive technology verification are scheduled.
